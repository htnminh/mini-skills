Wait — I don't understand where you've got to here. Re-pitch that: give me a little bit of context, talk in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, and use the project's own established terminology.

ASD-STE100 reminder:

- Use simple, common words, and give each word one consistent meaning.
- Keep terminology consistent.
- Prefer active voice and simple verb forms.
- Write one clear action or idea per sentence.
- Limit instructions to 20 words and descriptions to 25 words.
- Avoid contractions, jargon, ambiguity, long noun groups, and unnecessary synonyms.
- Place conditions before commands, and use imperative verbs for instructions.
- Verify vocabulary, sentence length, grammar, and technical accuracy before responding.
